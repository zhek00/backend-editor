[TITULO] The Asteroid Belt

Between Mars and Jupiter lies the asteroid belt, millions of rocky fragments left over from the birth of the solar system.

[TITULO] Ceres

Ceres is the largest object in the asteroid belt, a dwarf planet with bright white spots of salt on its surface.

[TITULO] Comets

Comets are frozen balls of ice and dust. When they come close to the Sun, they grow long glowing tails that stretch across the sky.

[TITULO] Pluto

Pluto is a small icy dwarf planet at the edge of the solar system. The New Horizons spacecraft revealed a giant heart-shaped plain of nitrogen ice.

[TITULO] The Kuiper Belt

Beyond Neptune lies the Kuiper Belt, a vast ring of frozen worlds. Arrokoth, a strange snowman-shaped rock, was visited by a spacecraft in 2019.

[TITULO] The Oort Cloud

Far beyond the Kuiper Belt, a giant cloud of icy objects may surround the whole solar system. It is the distant home of many comets.

[TITULO] Voyager

The Voyager probes, launched in 1977, have left the solar system. They now travel through interstellar space, carrying a golden record from Earth.

[TITULO] Into the Stars

Beyond the reach of the Sun, the Milky Way stretches out with billions of stars, and countless other worlds waiting to be discovered.
