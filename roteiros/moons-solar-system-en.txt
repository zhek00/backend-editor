[TITULO] The Moon

The Moon is Earth's closest companion. Its grey surface is covered in craters and dark plains, where astronauts walked for the first time in 1969.

[TITULO] Phobos and Deimos

Mars has two tiny moons, Phobos and Deimos. They look like potato-shaped rocks, covered in dust and craters.

[TITULO] Io

Io orbits Jupiter and is the most volcanic world in the solar system. Its yellow and orange surface is covered in sulfur and lava.

[TITULO] Europa

Europa is covered in a shell of white ice crossed by long cracks. Beneath the ice hides a huge ocean of liquid water.

[TITULO] Ganymede

Ganymede is the largest moon in the solar system, even bigger than the planet Mercury. Its surface mixes dark and bright icy regions.

[TITULO] Titan

Titan is Saturn's largest moon, hidden under a thick orange haze. It has lakes and rivers of liquid methane.

[TITULO] Enceladus

Enceladus is a small, bright white moon of Saturn. Giant geysers shoot water and ice into space from its south pole.

[TITULO] Triton

Triton orbits Neptune backwards, against the planet's spin. Its frozen pink and white surface has ice volcanoes.

[TITULO] Hundreds of Moons

The solar system has hundreds of moons, each one a different world waiting to be explored.
