[TITULO] The Sun

At the heart of our solar system burns the Sun, a giant star of glowing gas. Huge solar flares erupt from its surface, larger than entire planets.

[TITULO] Mercury

Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun. Its grey surface is covered in craters, and a year there lasts only eighty-eight days.

[TITULO] Venus

Venus is wrapped in thick yellow clouds. Beneath them, the surface is hot enough to melt lead, and the sky never clears.

[TITULO] Earth

Earth is the blue planet, with oceans, white clouds and green continents. It is the only place we know where life exists.

[TITULO] Mars

Mars is the red planet, covered in dust and deserts. It has the largest volcano in the solar system and deep canyons explored today by rovers.

[TITULO] Jupiter

Jupiter is the largest planet of all. Its colorful bands swirl with storms, and the Great Red Spot is a hurricane bigger than Earth.

[TITULO] Saturn

Saturn is surrounded by enormous rings of ice and rock. Seen up close, it glows golden against the darkness of space.

[TITULO] Uranus

Uranus is a pale blue ice giant. It spins on its side, like a ball rolling around the Sun.

[TITULO] Neptune

Neptune is the most distant planet, a deep blue world. Its winds are the fastest in the solar system, faster than the speed of sound.

[TITULO] Our Place in Space

Beyond Neptune, the solar system drifts on in silence, surrounded by billions of stars in the Milky Way.
